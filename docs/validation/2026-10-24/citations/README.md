# Citation evidence and final resolutions

`REPORT.md`, `summary.json`, and both citation matrices are the unchanged review
of manuscript commit `bccf7547280804c8cef915a3510b099c99e38cfd`. They account for
all 82 cited entries among 86 bibliography entries, and intentionally preserve
the findings against that earlier manuscript. Read `RESOLUTIONS.md` for the
subsequent corrections tied to the final manuscript/bibliography hashes.

The review retained primary-URL excerpts for 78 entries, added official,
publisher, or Crossref metadata for three entries, and used a macOS manual mirror
for one. These are reference/context checks, not full-text reading of every
external paper, exhaustive normalization of every metadata field, independent
replication of the external studies, or proof of Ironrun's correctness.

`critical-scope-evidence.json` retains short supporting quotations and source
URLs. `metadata-crosscheck.json` records the stronger targeted metadata checks.
`raw-evidence-inventory.json` hashes the original local research inputs, including
retained successes and a metadata-request failure. Those full third-party pages
are **not** duplicated in this repository; their hashes alone do not make the
inputs available offline. The matrices identify source URLs and retained-text
hashes so a reader can distinguish the archived review from a fresh retrieval.

`artifact-manifest.json` inventories this compact directory except itself. It is
a new packaging inventory, separate from the immutable original review records.
